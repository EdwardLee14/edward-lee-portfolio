# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers evaluating engineering candidates and freelance clients looking for a builder who can take a product from zero to one. They're scanning quickly, comparing against dozens of other portfolios, and deciding in seconds whether to dig deeper or move on.

## Product Purpose

Edward Lee's personal portfolio. It needs to do one thing: make the visitor want to reach out. The work speaks for itself (acquired startup, 300+ user iOS app, production systems at scale), so the site's job is to present it with enough craft and confidence that the design itself becomes proof of taste.

## Brand Personality

Professional, young, builder. Technically sharp but not showing off. Direct, not decorative. The tone is someone who ships real things and doesn't need to oversell it.

## Anti-references

- Card grids: identical rounded-corner cards repeated in rows. No cards as default container.
- Generic developer portfolio templates: gradient mesh backgrounds, floating particles, glassmorphism.
- Gradients as decoration. No gradient backgrounds, no gradient text.
- Rounded corners. Use sharp corners or minimal rounding (2-3px max).
- Too much empty whitespace. The page should feel dense with purpose, not padded for "breathing room."
- Generic sticky navbars with logo + links pattern.
- Braggy or self-congratulatory copy. Let the work and metrics speak.
- SaaS landing page energy: hero-metric templates, "trusted by" logos, pricing-page patterns.

## Design Principles

1. **The work is the proof.** Metrics, outcomes, and what got shipped. No adjective-heavy descriptions of how great you are.
2. **Density over padding.** Every pixel should earn its place. Tighter layouts that reward attention, not vast empty sections that waste scroll.
3. **Craft signals competence.** The portfolio is itself a piece of engineering. The animation, the typography, the attention to detail should make the visitor think: this person cares about quality.
4. **Sharp, not soft.** Hard edges, clear structure, decisive layouts. Nothing tentative or rounded off.
5. **Move.** A looping background animation gives the page life and energy. Motion should feel purposeful, not decorative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum for contrast ratios
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion for background animations
- Keyboard navigable
- Semantic HTML structure
